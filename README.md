# 🌟 Lumora AI

> **Conversations that Care. Technology that Understands.**

---

## 📖 About the Project

Lumora AI is a next-generation AI companion platform designed to support emotional wellbeing through empathetic conversations, long-term memory, intelligent voice interaction and responsible artificial intelligence.

The goal of this project is to create technology that helps people feel heard, supported and connected while respecting privacy, transparency and the importance of real human relationships.

---

## 🎯 Vision

To become one of the world's most trusted AI companion platforms by combining cutting-edge artificial intelligence with empathy, responsibility and thoughtful engineering.

---

## 🚀 Mission

To build an AI companion that supports meaningful conversations, encourages emotional wellbeing and contributes positively to society through responsible AI.
